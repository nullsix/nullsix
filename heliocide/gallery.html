HELIOCIDE — Card Gallery

Every card in the catalog, rendered at the exact sizes the duel screen uses on this device. The question this page exists to answer: does a card read at phone size? Scroll the board-size section first — that is the size you will actually be playing against.

Board size — the active creature

150 design px wide. Shown mid-fight: damaged, carrying energy, some with statuses — that is how they look when it matters. Grouped by type.

Bench slot — the tucked strip

Benched creatures use the same card, re-laid out into a 212 x 66 strip: sigil, name, HP, attached energy. Three of these stack beside the active card. Tap-to-zoom is how you read their attacks in play.

Hand size — the fanned cards

100 design px wide, pristine (as they come out of the deck). In play these overlap in a fan.

Zoomed — long-press any card in play

300 design px wide. The only size that carries flavour text and the attack lines.

Art plates — the ASCII pieces at reading size

Every piece is 32 x 16 printable ASCII characters, washed with its type's gradient (background-clip: text). This section ignores the switch above on purpose: the plate is always the ASCII, and the mark beside each name is always the procedural sigil, so the two art modes can be judged side by side. The switch decides which of them the cards themselves wear.

The four preset decks

15 cards each, drawn only from the deck's two energy types. Max two copies of a card.

Reading key

Energy types, cost pips, and every mark that can appear on a card.

HELIOCIDE is a toy riff on Bones of a Broken World imagery and carries zero canon weight. Cards render as pure CSS, inline SVG and printable ASCII — no image files anywhere in this page. Colours are the Thoughts of an Eaten Sun palette (cover + book site), per THEME.md.
